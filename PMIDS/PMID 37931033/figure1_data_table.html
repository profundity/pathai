Key gaps/flags established during analysis (see evidence_training_examples.md for full curator notes): Rescue-construct data exists only for AKT1, despite the paper's text stating all three paralogs were tested and concluding "any one paralog...can compensate for the other two." Endogenous pairwise-knockdown data (Table 1) independently supports AKT1 and AKT3 standalone sufficiency in all lines, and shows AKT2 standalone sufficiency is cell-line-dependent. Cell death (Table 2) was only tested for the triple-knockdown condition — reduced proliferation under partial knockdown was never shown to translate to actual cell death in any line.
